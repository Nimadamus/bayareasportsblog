The 2026-27 Warriors Roster and Depth Chart, Position by Position

Who starts, who comes off the bench, who is hurt, and what the roster looks like once you account for the two knees nobody wants to talk about.

Bay Area Sports Blog Staff · Warriors

A depth chart in the offseason is a projection with a short shelf life, so treat this as a running record. It gets updated as the roster moves.

Projected starters. Stephen Curry at point guard, Brandin Podziemski at shooting guard, Gui Santos at small forward, Draymond Green at power forward, and Kristaps Porzingis at centre. Read that lineup twice, because the two names most people would expect to see are not in it.

The two knees. Jimmy Butler and Moses Moody have both been left out of lineup projections because of knee injuries. Butler is the second-highest-paid player on the roster. A starting five that does not include him is not a plan, it is a contingency, and how quickly that changes is the single biggest variable in this team's season.

Guard. Curry, still the entire offense's centre of gravity at thirty-eight. Podziemski has grown into a real rotation piece rather than a project. Moody at $12.5 million is the sort of contract that looks fine when he plays and awkward when he does not.

Wing. Santos in the projected starting five is the story here, a player on a $4.6 million deal starting for a team paying two players a combined $119 million tells you exactly where the cap pressure landed. Butler when healthy changes this room entirely.

Big. Porzingis at centre, with Al Horford re-signed at $5.9 million behind him. Horford at this stage of his career is a specific kind of useful: he knows where to be, he can still shoot it, and he does not need plays run for him. On a roster this top-heavy, that is worth more than the number suggests.

Draymond. Still the defensive organiser, still on $27.6 million, still the player whose availability and temperament decide what the defence is capable of. There is no version of a good Warriors defence that does not run through him.

The rookie. Yaxel Lendeborg arrived in the draft. Rookie minutes on a team trying to win now are earned rather than given, and the Kuminga situation is the cautionary tale about how this coaching staff handles young players who are not immediately ready.

Where it runs thin. Everywhere behind the top six. The cap sheet, our cap piece goes through it in detail, means the back half of this roster is minimum contracts and hope. If Curry, Green or Porzingis misses time, there is no replacement on the books, only a redistribution of minutes to players who were not signed to play them.

The structural read is in the season outlook, the numbers behind Curry are on the Curry page, and everything else is on the Warriors hub.
