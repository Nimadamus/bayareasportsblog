Steph Curry Drops 41 in Overtime, and the Warriors Have Won Seven Straight

Forty one points, ten for ten at the line, the go ahead bucket in overtime, and a record nobody's ever had. He's 38. I can't stop smiling.

Bay Area Sports Blog Staff · Warriors
Warriors126
Final, OT
Magic119

Saturday night at Chase Center. Seven straight. We were 0 and 3 two weeks ago, in case anybody forgot.

I'm writing this with my voice half gone. I yelled so loud at the end of regulation that the dog left the room and didn't come back until overtime was over.

Steph Curry scored 41 on Saturday night. Thirteen of 19 from the floor, five threes, ten of ten at the line, six rebounds, six assists. He scored 12 points in the last two and a half minutes of regulation, he hit the floater that put us up two with 25 seconds left, and when Orlando tied it anyway he walked back out in overtime and finished the job. Warriors 126, Magic 119. Seven in a row.

Yet another one. I keep using that phrase in the group chat and my brother keeps telling me to stop, because every time I say it Steph goes out and does something dumber.

How the last few minutes went down

This shouldn't have been close. We were up 16 at the half, 57 to 41, and I was already halfway to the fridge. Then Orlando dropped 37 on us in the third quarter and led by one going into the fourth. Thirty seven! They didn't have Paolo Banchero, they didn't have Jalen Suggs, they didn't have Desmond Bane, and they still had us on the ropes. I'll get to why in a minute. It wasn't pretty.

Final 2:30, regulation

Steph takes it personally

Twelve points in a little over two minutes. He just decided. You could see it in the way he was bringing the ball up, no more swinging it around the perimeter waiting for something to open. He went and got it.

25 seconds left

The floater

Running floater in traffic, bodies everywhere, and it drops. Warriors up two. Kerr was still marveling at it afterward. So was I. I was on my feet with my hands on my head.

Final seconds

Jamal Cain ruins my night

Orlando misses, misses again, and Jamal Cain tips in the third try with about two seconds on the clock. 110 to 110. I sat down so hard I think I hurt something.

Start of OT

Kerr sits the stars

Steph and Draymond on the bench. Charles Bassey and Brandon Williams out there instead. I screamed at the TV. Then those two went and pushed us in front, and I owed the TV an apology.

2:24 left in OT

Da Silva from deep

Tristan da Silva hits a three and Orlando's up 118 to 117. Steph and Draymond had been back on the floor since the 3:48 mark. Here we go again.

1:38 left in OT

And one

Steph drives, floats it over Wendell Carter Jr., gets the foul, makes the free throw. 120 to 118, and we never trailed again. Carter went to the line at 1:15 with a chance to tie it and missed both. I'm not proud of how happy that made me.

Under a minute

Ballgame

Another Steph drive, another bucket, 122 to 118. Then Yaxel Lendeborg threw one down and hit two free throws and that was it. Forty one for Steph. Seven straight.

Sixteen, and nobody else has that

This is the part that got me. That was Steph's 16th game with 40 or more since he turned 35. Nobody in the history of this league has more. He was tied with LeBron at 15 coming in. Michael Jordan had 12.

Read that again. LeBron, who everybody calls the most durable superstar ever. Jordan. And the guy who's passed both of them is the one people kept telling me was too small and too fragile to last.

"It's kind of a weird one, but it makes me super proud of the work I've put in," Steph said after. That's such a Steph answer. A weird one. Okay man.

He also became just the seventh player ever with 26,000 points, 5,000 rebounds and 6,000 assists. And he's 66 of 67 from the line this season, which is the kind of number you stop believing after a while because it can't be real. It's real. One miss. All year.

Draymond put it best. "It never gets old," he said. "He always does something where it can't get old."

I've been watching this guy for 18 seasons. My brother and I used to argue about whether he'd ever stay healthy enough to matter. Now I'm watching him drop 41 in overtime at 38 years old. I don't know what to tell you. It doesn't get old.

Seven in a row, and every one of them different

Nuggets134 to 114 in Denver. Lendeborg 31 and 12
Wolves119 to 114 in Minnesota. Steph 44
Lakers112 to 95. Back from 20 plus down
Clippers124 to 112. Lendeborg 37, no Steph
Suns116 to 113. Down 17, Draymond tossed
Heat120 to 100. Steph 29, 22 after the half
Magic126 to 119 in OT. Steph 41

Go down that list and tell me what kind of team this is. Road blowouts, comebacks, a night without the stars, a fight night, a clean win, and now an overtime game where the best shooter who ever lived got the ball and everybody else got out of the way. It's the longest winning streak they've had since March of 2025.

We're 7 and 3. I wrote this team off after 0 and 3. I'm still a little embarrassed about that, and I'm going to keep being embarrassed for as long as it takes.

The rest of the guys showed up too

Lendeborg had 23 and eight rebounds and three blocks, and he hit a huge three in overtime. Every game I say I'm going to stop gushing about the rookie, and every game he gives me another reason not to. Brandin Podziemski went 4 of 5 from three for 15. Georges Niang had 11 and didn't miss from deep, three for three, and that's three straight games in double figures for a guy I complained about all summer. I'll eat that one.

And Draymond. Two points. Thirteen assists, a season high. That's the most Draymond line imaginable, and he ran the whole offense like he'd seen the game before it happened.

Oh, and Niang and Carter got into it in the second quarter and picked up double technicals. Draymond came flying in and security had to keep him out of it. He'd been thrown out of the Phoenix game on Monday, so I was holding my breath. He stayed in. Growth!

Okay, now the stuff that scares me

Nineteen turnovers. Nineteen! Against a team missing three of its best guys. Orlando took 113 shots, 33 more than us, and grabbed 24 offensive rebounds to our three. Three. I counted Cain putbacks in my sleep last night.

If Steph doesn't go nuclear in those last two minutes, we lose that game, and I'd be writing a very different column about the rebounding. I'm not going to pretend it didn't happen just because we won. Bigger, healthier teams are going to look at that tape and smile.

And I'll be honest, I still don't love sitting Steph and Draymond to start overtime. It worked. Bassey and Williams got us the lead. Steph even said the frustration on the bench was about wanting to be out there, not doubting the guys who went in. Fine. But my heart can't take that move every week, Steve.

Pelicans Monday, and I'm already nervous

New Orleans comes to Chase on Monday night. Eight straight would be a real number. I'm trying not to look at the standings every morning and I'm failing.

Two weeks ago I texted my brother that I'd see him at the trade deadline. Last night he texted me one word after Steph's and one. "Again?"

Yeah. Again.
