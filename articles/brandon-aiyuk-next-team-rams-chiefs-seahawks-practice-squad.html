Brandon Aiyuk in a Rams Helmet? A Chiefs One? Seattle? Oh, This Could Get Spicy.

He's reinstated, the Niners have until November 23 to cut him loose, and the three landing spots that scare me most are all still sitting on our schedule. I can't stop looking at the dates.

Bay Area Sports Blog Staff · 49ers

My buddy Dave texted me one word Monday night. Seattle. Then a skull.

I knew exactly what he meant, and I've been a little bit sick and a little bit giddy ever since, which is a combination I didn't know I had in me.

Quick catch up. The league reinstated Brandon Aiyuk on Monday and handed the Niners a two week roster exemption, which is a polite way of saying there's a clock on this now. It runs out November 23. The trade deadline came and went on Tuesday, so nobody's sending us a pick for him. He either goes on our 53 or he gets cut.

He's not going on our 53. Kyle said the reinstatement "means absolutely nothing to our team," and after the year those two have had, that might be the warmest thing he's said about it.

Which means that sometime in the next 12 days, a 28 year old receiver who went for 1,342 yards and made second team All-Pro in 2023 is going to be free to sign with anybody he wants. Anybody! And I made the mistake of pulling up our schedule.

Three dates I can't stop staring at

Let me be straight about what's real here. The Rams talk has actual legs. The Chiefs get mentioned right alongside them as a place a receiver could go rebuild his name fast. Seattle is mostly me and Dave scaring each other. But all three make football sense, and all three still have to play us.

Nov 29
Seahawks at Levi's
The one that keeps me up

Seattle's 5 and 4 and chasing us. We beat them 32 to 29 up there in October and I aged a year doing it. They come to Santa Clara six days after that exemption runs out. Six days! Nobody's tied him to Seattle yet, I want that on the record. But picture number 11 jogging out of the visitors tunnel in that highlighter green, knowing every check and every signal Kyle's got.

Dec 13
Rams at Levi's
The one that makes the most sense

Puka Nacua is headed to injured reserve with a sprained MCL and PCL plus turf toe. Davante Adams was already on IR with the hamstring. Their top three right now are Konata Mumpfield, Tutu Atwell and Jordan Whittington, and they didn't make a move at the deadline. They're 5 and 4 and McVay needs a body who can win outside. I hate how clean this fit is.

Dec 27
49ers at Chiefs, Arrowhead
The one that would be the cruelest

His knee went against Kansas City. October 20, 2024, and he hasn't played a football game since. Now imagine his first big one back is for them, against us, two days after Christmas. The Chiefs are 6 and 2 and they clearly aren't happy with their receivers, because they just traded for Josh Downs with Xavier Worthy nursing a hamstring and Tyquan Thornton on IR. Andy Reid with a motivated Aiyuk and a month to draw stuff up. No thank you.

He's not walking in as anybody's number one

This is the part people are skipping. Whoever gets him isn't handing him a starting job on a Wednesday. The road back almost certainly starts on a practice squad, and it probably goes like this.

  • FirstThe Niners let him go. Because the deadline's passed, he goes through waivers before he's a free agent. Nobody's claiming what's left of a four year, $120 million contract, so he clears.
  • ThenSomebody stashes him. Practice squad. No draft pick, hardly any money, no risk. The word going around is at least one NFC team has already talked about doing exactly this.
  • NextGame day call ups. A team can bump a practice squad guy up three times before it has to clear a real roster spot for him. That's three Sundays to find out what he's got.
  • LastThe 53. If the legs are there, he's on an active roster by December. Look at those three dates again.

And I get why it has to be that way. It's been more than two years since he played in a game. He says he's back to 100 percent, he passed his physical, and Drew Rosenhaus is out there saying "I definitely expect him to play this season and there is interest in him." Fine. Passing a physical and getting off press coverage in December are different sports.

Okay, the boring answer

I'm a fan, not a liar, so I'll say it. The favorite is probably none of my three. Rosenhaus guessed his guy lands with a team that already knows him, from college or from his time here, and that points pretty hard at Washington. Jayden Daniels was his quarterback at Arizona State. Their GM, Adam Peters, was in our building when we drafted him. And this summer he was out there saying Go Commanders for the whole world to see.

We already played Washington. Beat them 23 to 17 on a Monday night. If that's where he ends up, we probably don't see him again unless it's January, and honestly? I could live with that. It's the tidy ending.

I just don't trust tidy with this guy. Nothing about the last two years has been tidy. The $13 million the league says he owes us still isn't settled, and Rosenhaus keeps saying that fight is separate from where he plays. So the money drags on in one room while he's running routes for somebody else in another.

Do I actually want this?

Kind of. Is that awful?

I spent a whole summer in 2024 defending him to people at work. I've still got the jersey, it's in the hall closet behind the vacuum, and I haven't been able to throw it out or put it on. Some part of me wants him to be great again, because the guy who toasted half the league in 2023 was one of my favorite players we've had since Kyle got here. I just want him great in the other conference, on a Thursday, when I'm not watching.

And we're fine, by the way. We're 7 and 1. We just went and got Keenan Allen for a sixth round pick. Nobody in that locker room is losing sleep over this.

I am, though. I'm supposed to be thinking about Dallas on Sunday and instead I'm refreshing the transaction wire like it owes me money. If it says Seattle, Dave is never going to let me hear the end of it.

How we got here is in the day he showed up in Santa Clara with his agent. The rest of the season is on the schedule hub, the receiver room is on the depth chart, and everything else lives on the 49ers hub.
