This page is the schedule.

Dates, opponents, home or away, and the result once the game has actually been played. Nothing on it is a prediction, and nothing on it is an opinion about whether this team is any good.

If you want the argument about what this roster is and what it can realistically do, that's a different page and I wrote it already: the 2026-27 outlook. This one is for checking when they play.

Next up: at LA Clippers, Sunday October 4 at 4:00 pm, Intuit Dome. That one is preseason. No regular season games have been played yet.

What this schedule looks like

Counted straight off the league listing, not off a vibe:

  • Opening night is Wednesday October 21, at Los Angeles Lakers.
  • The home opener is Friday October 23 against Memphis Grizzlies.
  • 40 home games and 40 on the road, finishing April 11.
  • Longest homestand 7 games, longest road trip 6.
  • 15 back to backs on the calendar as published.

The thing I look for every year is the February stretch, because that is where a tired team stops covering for itself. You can see it above before it happens to you.
