Mac Jones vs the Seahawks: Brock's Out, and I'm Weirdly Fired Up

Purdy's ankle didn't heal in time. Mac gets the champs at Levi's on Sunday. I spent Wednesday panicking about this exact moment, and now that it's here, I kind of love it.

Bay Area Sports Blog Staff · 49ers · November 27, 2026
Admit one nervous fan Seahawks at 49ers Sunday · 1:25 PM · Levi's Stadium · FOX
QB Mac Jones starts

So it's official. Brock's out.

Kyle made the call after practice Friday, and honestly I'd known since Thursday when Brock didn't throw again. Three straight days of nothing. You don't need a medical degree to read that. The ankle turned out to be worse than we were told on Wednesday, too. Kyle called it "more mid than low, which is a little bit worse," and said it just "didn't heal up as fast as we wanted, so had to shut him down."

No surgery. He's "pretty optimistic" about the Giants next week. Fine. Good. I'll take it.

But I want to tell you what happened to me this afternoon, because I didn't expect it. I was standing in the kitchen picking at leftover stuffing straight out of the Tupperware, read the news, and instead of the stomach drop I had on Wednesday, I felt something closer to... fine? Better than fine. I felt like I wanted to watch Mac Jones go beat Seattle.

My brother texted "we're cooked." I texted back "we went 5 and 3 with him last year, relax." He sent the clown emoji. We'll see who's the clown on Sunday night.

Passes thrown in 2026 so far: one. Completed it. Five yards. Perfect season intact.

Look, I know what the national guys are going to say on Sunday morning. Purdy's leading the league in passer rating at 118.2, he's at nine yards an attempt, he just went 21 of 26 for 255 and three scores on that ankle in Mexico City, and now the 9 and 1 Niners hand the ball to a backup. Cue the graphic with the sad trombone.

Except Mac isn't some guy they found at a bus stop. He started eight games for us last year when Brock was beat up and went 5 and 3. He knows the offense, he knows the receivers, he knows exactly how Kyle wants the ball out on time. And Kyle said it himself today: "We went through this a lot with him last year, so everyone's got a ton of confidence in Mac."

Remember the trade deadline? Everybody and their uncle wanted us to flip him for a pick. He's in the last year of his deal. It made sense on paper. I wrote at the time that we had to keep him, and Kyle basically said the same thing this week, that when you've got a backup "who you believe could be one of the better starters in this league, that's a hell of a deal."

A hell of a deal. That's this week. That's exactly this week.

What Mac has to do Sunday, in order of how loud I'll yell

  1. Get the ball out before the pass rush arrives. Seattle's defense is the reason they've got a ring. They're stingy, they hit, and the second a quarterback holds it, he's on his back. Mac's best stuff last year was rhythm and timing, quick game, hitting the first open guy. Don't hunt for the hero throw. Take the eight yards. I'll clap for the eight yards.
  2. Lean on Mike Evans if he plays. Evans came back last week from the Achilles and went for 109 and a touchdown against the Vikings. He's questionable, limited Thursday and Friday, and I'm refreshing the inactives list Sunday morning like it's a lottery drawing. Jordan Watkins is already out with the groin, so we're thinner than I'd like on the outside. If Evans is out there, throw it up to him. He's six foot five. That's the whole plan.
  3. Do. Not. Throw. It. To. Them. Six picks in eight starts last year isn't a disaster, but against this defense one bad decision in our own end turns a game we should win into a coin flip. Punting is fine. I promise I won't boo a punt.
  4. Let the run game carry him. We didn't go 9 and 1 on Brock's arm alone. Pound it, play action off it, and let Mac throw into the space it creates. That's Kyle's offense when it's working, and that's how a backup wins in it.
  5. Have some fun with it. Mac's a fiery guy. He's been standing on the sideline all season holding a tablet. This is his shot to remind the whole league why the Niners wouldn't trade him. I want to see him yelling at the line again.

He's saying the right stuff, at least. "It's an amazing defense," he said this week, and that going up against them "is going to be a huge challenge, and I'm looking forward to it." I believe him. You could hear it.

And yes, it's the Seahawks, of course it is

The defending champions. Six and four, fighting for a playoff spot, coming off a bye with two weeks to stare at film of us, and getting Sam Darnold back after he missed two games with the quad and the ribs. They'd love nothing more than to come into our building and drag us back toward the pack.

I still haven't gotten over January. 41 to 6 in the divisional round. I don't watch the highlights. I'm not going to talk about it any more than that.

We did go up there in Week 5 and win 32 to 29, which felt like therapy. A sweep would feel like a full recovery. Beating them with the backup? At Levi's? With Darnold coming back and the whole country expecting a letdown? I'd frame that one. I'd frame the box score and hang it next to my Dwight Clark poster.

Five and three last year. One for one this year. Nobody's handing Seattle this game.

The stuff that actually scares me

Not Mac. The health stuff around him. Colton McKivitz is done for the year with the ankle and went on IR, so the right side of the line is getting reshuffled the same week our starting quarterback is out. Dre Greenlaw's questionable again with the neck. Upton Stout's questionable. Austen Pleasants might come off IR, and they'll decide Saturday.

That's a lot of moving parts for a game against a team that just had two weeks to rest. If we lose this one, it won't be because Mac Jones isn't good enough. It'll be because we were patching holes all week and the champs smelled it.

The one good bit of injury news: Nate Hobbs is back off IR. I'll take every corner we can get with Darnold slinging it again.

My call

Final, Sunday night, in my head 49ers 24, Seahawks 20 Mac throws two, nobody throws a pick for us, the defense gets Darnold twice, and Brock limps in next week against the Giants to a 10 and 1 team.

The books still have us around a field goal favorite even with Brock out. That tells you something. Vegas doesn't think we fall apart, and neither do I.

I'm wearing the Purdy jersey anyway. Not out of disrespect to Mac. It's in the drawer until Brock's back on the field, superstitions are superstitions, so I'm going with the old Montana throwback I got for my 30th. Feels right for a guy who's been waiting his turn.

Go get 'em, Mac. Make my brother eat that clown emoji.
