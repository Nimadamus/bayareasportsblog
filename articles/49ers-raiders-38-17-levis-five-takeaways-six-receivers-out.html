Six Receivers Down, the Raiders Back in the Bay, and We Still Hung 38 on Them

No Mike Evans. No Deebo. No Christian Kirk. No Pearsall, no Stribling, no Aiyuk. Didn't matter. The 49ers ran the Raiders off the Levi's Stadium grass 38 to 17, the defense took the ball away five times in one half, and we are 7 and 1.

Bay Area Sports Blog Staff · 49ers

I need a second. I've been yelling at my TV all afternoon and my voice is doing that thing where it cracks like I'm fourteen again.

The Raiders came back to the Bay. First time since they packed up and moved to Las Vegas, plenty of silver and black in our building, and they left with a 38 to 17 beating and five turnovers to think about on the flight home. That's the rivalry, folks. They can move the team. They can't move the result.

And here's the part I can't get over. Go look at who was NOT out there.

Not in uniform

  • Mike Evans, inactive with the Achilles and ribs
  • Deebo Samuel, inactive with the hamstring
  • Christian Kirk, on IR Saturday with a meniscus
  • Ricky Pearsall, done for the year after PCL surgery
  • De'Zhaun Stribling, still on IR after ankle surgery
  • Brandon Aiyuk, his locker's been gone since August

Who actually caught the ball

  • Jordan Watkins, 4 catches, 86 yards, first NFL touchdown
  • Demarcus Robinson, 3 for 22 in his first game back from IR
  • George Kittle, a 37 yard catch to set up a score
  • Christian McCaffrey, 2 for 19 and a touchdown
  • KhaDarel Hodge, called up from the practice squad, 17 yard grab

Six receivers you'd have penciled in back in July, gone. The 49ers announced three of them out before the weekend and everybody I know spent Saturday saying some version of "well, this is the one we lose." We did not lose. We won by 21 and it could've been more.

How the afternoon went

1st quarter

Two drives, two touchdowns

The opening drive goes 69 yards and McCaffrey punches it in from the 1. Next possession goes 89 yards, Purdy hits Kittle for 37, then Purdy keeps it himself from the 1. It's 14 to 0 before the Raiders know what hit them.

2nd quarter

Purdy's 100th

A 12 yard touchdown pass to McCaffrey for a 21 to 7 lead, and it was the 100th touchdown pass of Brock Purdy's career. More on that in a minute. Halftime, 21 to 10.

17 seconds in

Jaden Dugger takes it to the house

First Raiders snap of the second half. Kirk Cousins looks for Tre Tucker, the rookie linebacker jumps it, and he's gone. His first career interception is a pick six. You can't write it better.

Late 3rd

Watkins, finally

Third down, Purdy throws it over the middle, and Jordan Watkins goes up in traffic and takes it from 37 yards out. After the one they took away from him against Atlanta, this kid earned that one twice. 35 to 17.

4th quarter

The defense keeps taking

Renardo Green picks Cousins in the end zone. Upton Stout punches the ball out of Michael Mayer's hands at the goal line and Fred Warner falls on it. Malik Mustapha picks off the last Raiders drive. Final, 38 to 17.

Five takeaways. In one half.

We came into this game with seven takeaways in seven games. Seven. I've complained about it on this site more than once. Then this defense goes out and grabs five in the second half alone: three interceptions off Cousins and two fumbles. According to the team, it's the first time the 49ers forced five turnovers in a game since 2014, and the first time they got five in a single half since 2009.

Fred Warner punched one out of Brock Bowers' arms and Dre Greenlaw jumped on it. Greenlaw led the team with eight tackles. Warner has five forced fumbles already this year. And the Raiders' run game? Twenty four yards. Total. 1.6 a carry. The fewest rushing yards this defense has allowed since 2015.

And Nick Bosa was back after missing five games. And DeForest Buckner played his first game in a 49ers uniform since 2019, which still feels fake to type. Buckner said afterward, "It's like we never skipped a beat." Big man, we noticed.

This offense is a problem for the rest of the league

Let's talk about the scary part, because if you're a fan of any other team in the NFC you should be scared.

This offense put up 38 without its two biggest names at receiver and four more guys who were supposed to be in the rotation. You know what Shanahan did? He just ran the football down their throats. 43 carries for 190 yards, a season high, and the most rushing attempts this team has had since 2022. McCaffrey went 18 for 68 with a touchdown on the ground and another through the air.

That receiving touchdown moved McCaffrey past Marshall Faulk for the most TD catches by a running back in the Super Bowl era. He's also run for a touchdown in seven straight games, which the team says is the longest single season streak by any 49er ever. The man is 30 years old and playing like a cheat code.

And Purdy. 17 of 25 for 232 yards, two touchdown passes, 40 yards on the ground and a rushing touchdown. That 100th touchdown pass came in his 57th career game, the fastest anyone in franchise history has gotten there. Joe Montana needed 86. Steve Young needed 81. I'll let that sit with the people who still call him a system quarterback. If you want the full argument, it's on the where Purdy actually ranks page.

Here's what I keep coming back to. When Evans and Deebo come back, and both are expected back, this is what the offense gets added to it. A run game that can carry a game by itself, a quarterback who doesn't care who's open, Kittle, McCaffrey, and now a Watkins who looks like he belongs. Trent Williams said it best about him: "Jordan, his maturation is showing right in front our eyes."

The one that hurt

Gracen Halton got carted off in the fourth quarter, and it's a season ending left ankle injury. The rookie scored a fumble return touchdown at Seattle back in October, and now he's done. That one stings, and it took a little of the fun out of the last few minutes. Renardo Green also left with a back injury and didn't come back. Keep an eye on the depth chart this week.

So where are we

7 and 1. A fifth straight win coming off a bye. A 5 and 0 start at Levi's for the first time since 1998. Three straight wins over the Raiders and six of the last seven. Shanahan, of course, is not getting carried away. "We've got a lot more work to do before we can say that," he said when somebody asked if this defense is Super Bowl good. Nick Bosa was a little less careful: "Yeah, we have the guys to do it."

I'm with Bosa.

Next up is Dallas on the road next Sunday at 1:25, then the Vikings in Mexico City. The trade deadline is Tuesday and Shanahan said they're always looking to upgrade. Honestly? After today I'm not sure what they'd even need. Everything week to week is on the schedule hub, the Raiders side of this is in our Raiders season preview, and the rest lives on the 49ers hub.

The Raiders came to our house with their fans and their history. They got Jaden Dugger, Jordan Watkins and five turnovers. Come back anytime.
