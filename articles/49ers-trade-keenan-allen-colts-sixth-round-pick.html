Keenan Allen Is a 49er, and All It Cost Us Was a Sixth Round Pick

Six Pro Bowls. 1,095 career catches. 34 years old and not what he used to be. I don't care. Half our receiver room is hurt and John Lynch just got a pro's pro for a 2027 sixth. Sign me up.

Bay Area Sports Blog Staff · 49ers

Okay. Deep breath.

On Sunday night I sat here and typed that I wasn't sure what this team even needed at the trade deadline. I'd like that one back, please, because John Lynch just went out and got Keenan Allen.

Keenan. Allen. The guy who spent a decade in a Chargers uniform making cornerbacks look silly on third down. The guy from Cal. He's ours now, and according to multiple reports all it took was a 2027 sixth round pick going to the Colts. A sixth! In 2027! That pick is a guy who gets cut in August. We just turned it into one of the best route runners of his generation.

Yes, I know how old he is

Let me get ahead of the comments. He's 34. He is not the 2017 version, and nobody in Santa Clara thinks he is.

His numbers in Indianapolis this year: 40 catches, 351 yards and one touchdown in eight games, four of them starts. That's 8.8 yards a catch, the lowest of his career. A year ago with the Chargers he still caught 81 balls. This year, on a Colts team going nowhere and selling off everything that isn't bolted down, it's been a whole lot of five yard outs.

So is he a little washed? Kind of, yeah. I'm a fan, not a liar. He isn't running by anybody anymore.

But here's the thing. He never really ran by anybody. Keenan Allen's whole career is being open by two steps on third and 6 because he sold the corner something that wasn't there. That doesn't age the way speed does. And 40 catches in eight games is five a game, from a 34 year old, on a bad offense. I'll take five catches a game right now. Honestly I'd take three.

Have you seen our receiver room?

Because that's what this is really about. Ricky Pearsall is done for the year after PCL surgery. Christian Kirk just went on IR with a meniscus. De'Zhaun Stribling has been on IR with the ankle since Week 1 and they're targeting December. Mike Evans has missed two straight with the Achilles and the ribs and sounds questionable at best for this week. Deebo has missed three with the hamstring, though he might be back Sunday. And Brandon Aiyuk is not walking through that door.

We just beat the Raiders 38 to 17 with Jordan Watkins, Demarcus Robinson in his first game back, and a guy called up from the practice squad. I loved every second of it. I also know you can't live like that until January.

And don't forget, Lynch already tried this once. Nine days ago he went after Jauan Jennings, and he said no and stayed in Minnesota. So he got back on the phone, called the same Colts front office that sent us DeForest Buckner for a fourth round pick, and came away with a six time Pro Bowler for even less. Indianapolis is having a garage sale and we keep showing up early.

Stuff you're probably wondering

What did it actually cost?

Rd 6one 2027 draft pick

That's the whole price, per the reports. Allen is on a one year deal worth $3.32 million that he signed with the Colts in August, and he's a free agent after the season. No long term anything. If it doesn't work, we lost a pick we would've forgotten we owned.

When does he play?

Sundayat Dallas, 1:25 Pacific

This is the funny part. Allen played the Cowboys LAST Sunday. He caught six passes for 32 yards for the Colts in a loss to Dallas, and now he gets them again seven days later in a different uniform at AT&T Stadium. Two weeks, two teams, same opponent. Whether he's got enough of Shanahan's playbook down by then to play more than a handful of snaps, I have no idea. That offense is not something you pick up on a flight. But the man has 1,095 catches. He'll figure out where to stand.

Where does he fit when everybody's healthy?

3rd downthat's the job

If Evans and Deebo come back, Allen isn't the first option and he doesn't need to be. He's the guy Brock Purdy looks at on third and 5 when Kittle is doubled. Purdy throws on time to a spot. Allen has made a career out of being at the spot. I think those two are going to like each other a lot.

Wait, isn't he a Bay Area guy?

Cal2010 to 2012

He is. Allen played his college ball in Berkeley and left with the school record for catches, 205 of them. The Chargers took him in the third round in 2013, pick 76, and he's still that franchise's all time leader in receptions. So call it a homecoming. And to be straight with you, he was arrested in Indianapolis in August on misdemeanor DWI charges, and that case hasn't gone away. It's part of the story and I'm not going to pretend it isn't.

This is what a contender does

We're 7 and 1. Purdy is playing like an MVP. The defense just took the ball away five times in one half. This is not the year to sit on your hands and hope the hamstrings heal. This is the year you go grab every useful veteran who wants a ring, and the word is Allen wanted out of Indy so he could chase one.

Is he going to put up 1,200 yards? No. Is he going to catch a third and 7 in January that keeps a drive alive while somebody younger and faster is standing on the sideline in street clothes? I'd bet a sixth round pick on it.

Oh wait. We already did.

Welcome home, Keenan. The receiver room is on the depth chart, who's hurt this week is on the injury report, Dallas week is on the schedule hub, and everything else lives on the 49ers hub.
